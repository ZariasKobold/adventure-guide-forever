# Adventure Guide: Forever

Brings the retail Adventure Guide back to World of Warcraft: Forever.

## Install
Copy the `AdventureGuideForever` folder into `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\`.

## Use
- **Shift+J**, the Adventure Guide button on the menu bar, the addon compartment (the addon
  list button by the minimap), or `/ag` opens it. `/ag help` lists the commands.
- Tabs: **Suggested Content** (fits your level), **Dungeons**, **Raids**, **Gear**,
  **Suggested Gear**, and **Wishlist**.
- Each boss has **Overview**, **Abilities**, **Loot**, and **Model** sections.
- Shift-click loot to link it in chat. Ctrl-click to preview it.
- **Options** live with other addons' settings, in Esc > Options > AddOns > Adventure Guide
  Forever (the guide's Options button and `/ag options` open them there). They turn each
  feature below on or off and set the key that opens the guide.

## The guide comes to you
- **Drops from:** every item tooltip, anywhere in the game, shows which bosses drop it and
  how often.
- **Briefing cards:** entering a dungeon shows a card with the route, the bosses, a tip, and
  your wishlist items there. Targeting a boss shows its card: who it is, the tip for your
  role, and the abilities to watch for. Drag a card to move it. "Turn off" on the card (or
  `/ag cards`, `/ag bosscards`) switches them off.
- **Gear tab:** every boss drop and quest reward, filtered by slot, armor or weapon type,
  stat (sorted by it), quality, and required level (your level and the ten below it, unless
  you type a range). "Only what my class can use" hides armor and weapons you can't equip.
  Each item shows its stats, item level, required level, and where it comes from.
  **Dungeon** narrows it to one dungeon or raid; "Where I am now" follows you in. With a
  dungeon picked, the list shows every level, puts what's best for your spec first, and
  says what's an upgrade over what you're wearing.
- **Suggested Gear tab:** the best piece per slot you can get from dungeon bosses and
  quests at your level, for your class and spec, with what each one beats in what you're
  wearing ("+12 upgrade", "Equipped"). Pick another class, spec, or level to plan ahead
  or for an alt, and wishlist the whole set in one click. The stat weights are shown on
  the page: simple suggestions, not the last word.
- **Search:** the box at the top of the guide (or `/ag find <name>`) finds dungeons,
  bosses, gear (boss drops and quest rewards), quests, and abilities by name. Click a
  result to jump to it.
- **Quests:** each dungeon's overview lists its quests for your faction, easiest first,
  with where each one starts (and map coordinates), which chain it belongs to, its
  rewards, and a check mark once you've done it. Quest rewards show up in item tooltips
  and the wishlist too. Hover one for its quest tooltip. The dungeon card tells you
  how many you haven't done yet.
- **Wishlist:** click the star next to any loot. Dungeon cards show how many wishlist items
  are inside, the Wishlist tab lists them, and you get an alert when one drops.
- **Want to track where you've been?** Distance traveled, the step counter by the minimap,
  and how many times you've visited each dungeon, raid, and zone live in their own addon,
  **[Adventure Forever Pedometer](https://github.com/ZariasKobold/adventure-forever-pedometer)**.
  Install it next to this one and it brings over everything this guide counted before.

## Where the data comes from
- `Data\Content\<instance>.lua`: hand-written text (intros, boss overviews, abilities
  with role alerts). Edit these freely. One file per instance, all in the same format.
- `Data\Generated\Wowhead.lua`: Forever NPC IDs and loot tables from Wowhead's Forever
  database. Generated by `tools\build_wowhead.py`, so never edit it by hand.
- `Data\Registry.lua` merges them when the addon loads. Its header lists the rules.
